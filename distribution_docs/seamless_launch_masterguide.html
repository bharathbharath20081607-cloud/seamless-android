2. Open Source Strategy & GitHub Setup

When you publish your entire codebase to GitHub, following these precise steps guarantees zero intellectual property friction.

A. The Required License: GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3)

Because you incorporated code from Briar (Bramble synchronization and Tor hidden service engine) and Signal (Double Ratchet algorithm in com.seamless.android.crypto), both of which are licensed under GPLv3, your entire derivative codebase must also be released under GPLv3.

The Copyleft Rule Works in Your Favor
Bitchat's MIT license allows merging into GPLv3 projects freely. Releasing Seamless under GPLv3 ensures no corporation can take your work closed-source without your permission.

B. Repository Sanitization Checklist (Before Hitting "Public")

  • Strip Keystores & Passwords: Ensure no .jks or .keystore files or passwords exist in gradle.properties or Git history.
  • Verify .gitignore: Confirm /build, .gradle, local.properties, and Android Studio cache files are untracked.
  • Check Package Name: Ensure com.seamless.droid is the active ID across all manifests.
  • Root LICENSE File: Replace the existing placeholder with the standard GPL-3.0 license text.

B. The 2 Critical Google Play Declarations

1. Background Bluetooth & Location Declaration

Because Android requires ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION to scan for nearby Bluetooth devices and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE to maintain mesh links when the screen is locked, Google Play will ask for a justification video and explanation.

Copy-Paste Justification for Play Console:
"Seamless is a decentralized peer-to-peer emergency communication utility. It maintains an ad-hoc local mesh network over Bluetooth Low Energy. The foreground service and Bluetooth scanning permissions are core requirements to discover nearby peers and relay encrypted packets while the device is in the pocket or screen is off. Location permission is required strictly by Android's BLE discovery API; no GPS location data is tracked, stored, or transmitted to any server."

2. Cryptography & US Export (EAR) Questionnaire

When the Play Console asks: "Does your app use encryption?"

  • Select: YES.
  • Select Exemption: Yes, the app qualifies for an exemption under Category 5, Part 2 (Mass Market / Publicly Available Open Source Encryption - License Exception ENC).
  • Because your code is 100% public on GitHub, it is legally exempt from US export licensing.
